Questions

Short answers.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The calculators run on your device and work fully offline. No data leaves your phone.

Is there a subscription?

No. Lite is free with 3 calculators. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks all 20 calculators and the technical reference.

Which units and languages are supported?

US field units and Metric, switched with one tap. English, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Dutch, Norwegian, Danish and Russian.

Is it available on iPhone?

Yes. Mud Engineer Pro and Lite are on the App Store (Lite) for iPhone, and on Google Play for Android.

Can I rely on the results for critical decisions?

The apps are a calculation aid built on standard published formulas, and every formula is documented in the technical reference. Always cross-check against your company procedures, your drilling program and your mud company's reported values.

How do I get Mud Engineer Enterprise?

Enterprise is a PC application for companies. Contact us for a demo and licensing.
