It is written to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, state privacy legislation in the United States (including CCPA/CPRA), and equivalent privacy laws in other regions where the apps are distributed worldwide.

In one sentence: Mud Engineer Pro runs entirely on your device, processes no personal data, and needs no internet connection. Mud Engineer Lite works the same way for its calculators, but is free to use and shows ads through Google AdMob, which requires an internet connection and involves limited data sharing with Google as described in Section 3 below.

2. What Data the App Processes — Both Apps

Neither app collects, stores, transmits, sells, or shares any of the following, whether directly or through a third party:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other identity information
  • Calculation inputs or outputs (mud weights, pressures, well data, or any other figures you enter) — these stay on your device and are never transmitted anywhere, in either app
  • Location data
  • Photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or any other device sensor or permission

The only information either app keeps is stored locally on your own device, using your browser's or device's standard local storage mechanism, and is never transmitted anywhere: your selected language and unit system (US/Metric), and up to 10 recent calculation results per calculator, saved automatically so you can refer back to them during a shift. You can erase this at any time via the app's "Clear All" history control, your device's app-storage settings, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising — Mud Engineer Lite Only

Mud Engineer Pro has no advertising and this section does not apply to it.

Mud Engineer Lite is free to use and is supported by advertising delivered through Google AdMob. To show ads, the Lite app:

  • Requires an internet connection (the calculators themselves still work fully offline; only the ads need connectivity)
  • May share device and advertising identifiers with Google for the purpose of selecting and delivering ads, and measuring ad performance
  • Shows a consent management prompt on first launch (for users in the EEA, UK, and applicable jurisdictions) letting you choose whether to allow personalized advertising; non-personalized ads may still be shown if you decline

Google's own handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and, for advertising-specific disclosures, Google's How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services page. We do not receive or store your personal data from AdMob ourselves — the sharing happens between your device and Google directly.

Your calculation inputs and results are never shared with Google or any advertiser, in either app.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Mud Engineer Pro processes no personal data as defined under GDPR Art. 4(1), so no legal basis under Art. 6 is required. For Mud Engineer Lite's advertising (Section 3), the legal basis is your consent, collected via the in-app consent form for EEA/UK users; where consent is not required by local law, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in funding a free product through advertising. The limited local-only storage described in Section 2 occurs solely on your own device under your own control and is not transmitted to the Developer in either app.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR & International Laws

For Mud Engineer Pro, we hold no personal data about you, so most data-subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection under GDPR Art. 15–21) have nothing to act on. For Mud Engineer Lite, any personal data connected to advertising (such as an advertising identifier) is processed by Google as described in Section 3 — you can exercise your rights over that data directly with Google, and you can withdraw ad-consent at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings, which resets the consent form. For any other privacy question, contact us at mudengineerpro.tech@outlook.de. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

6. Children's Privacy

Both apps are professional engineering reference tools intended for drilling fluid engineers and related industry professionals. They are not directed at children, and in any event, we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or under 16/18 depending on jurisdiction), in either app.

7. International Data Transfers

Mud Engineer Pro: not applicable — no data is transmitted anywhere. Mud Engineer Lite: any transfer of advertising-related data to Google is governed by Google's own global compliance mechanisms (including EU Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable), as described in Google's Privacy Policy linked in Section 3.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Neither app uses cookies or web beacons. Mud Engineer Lite's ad delivery may use comparable identifiers (such as mobile advertising IDs) as described in Section 3, which is not a cookie but serves a similar function for ad measurement.

9. Third-Party Services

Mud Engineer Pro integrates no third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud services. Mud Engineer Lite integrates Google AdMob as described in Section 3, and no other third-party service. If you download either app through an app store (Apple App Store, Google Play, or another distributor), that store's own privacy policy governs the purchase/download transaction itself — this Privacy Policy covers only the apps' own behavior after installation.

10. Data Retention

Local preferences and saved calculation history (Section 2) remain on your device until you clear them or uninstall the app, in both apps. We hold no copy of this data and cannot retrieve, back up, or delete it on your behalf, since it never reaches us. Advertising-related data in Mud Engineer Lite is retained by Google under its own retention policies.

11. Security

Because no calculation data leaves your device in either app, the primary risk surface is your own device's security, which remains your responsibility (e.g., device passcode, OS updates). We do not operate any server or database that could be breached in connection with either app.

12. Changes to This Policy
