It is written to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, state privacy legislation in the United States (including CCPA/CPRA), and equivalent privacy laws in other regions where the apps are distributed worldwide.

In one sentence: Both Mud Engineer Pro and Mud Engineer Lite run entirely on your device, process no personal data, contain no advertising, and need no internet connection. The only difference between them is that Pro is a paid, one-time purchase with the full set of calculators, while Lite is free with a limited set of calculators.

1. Data Controller

Rig Floor Tech
Germany
Contact Email: mudengineerpro.tech@outlook.de

2. What Data the Apps Process

Neither app collects, stores, transmits, sells, or shares any of the following, whether directly or through a third party:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other identity information
  • Calculation inputs or outputs (mud weights, pressures, well data, or any other figures you enter) — these stay on your device and are never transmitted anywhere, in either app
  • Location data
  • Advertising identifiers or any other tracking identifiers
  • Photos, contacts, microphone, camera, or any other device sensor or permission

The only information either app keeps is stored locally on your own device, using your device's standard local storage mechanism, and is never transmitted anywhere: your selected language and unit system (US/Metric), and up to 10 recent calculation results per calculator, saved automatically so you can refer back to them during a shift. You can erase this at any time via the app's "Clear All" history control, your device's app-storage settings, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Advertising

Neither Mud Engineer Pro nor Mud Engineer Lite contains any advertising. The apps do not use Google AdMob or any other advertising network, do not show ads, and do not share any device or advertising identifiers with advertisers or any third party.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Both apps process no personal data as defined under GDPR Art. 4(1), so no legal basis under Art. 6 is required. The limited local-only storage described in Section 2 occurs solely on your own device under your own control and is not transmitted to the Developer in either app.

5. Your Rights Under GDPR & International Laws

Because we hold no personal data about you for either app, most data-subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection under GDPR Art. 15–21) have nothing to act on. The local preferences and saved history described in Section 2 are entirely under your control on your own device and can be cleared at any time from within the app or your device settings. For any privacy question, contact us at mudengineerpro.tech@outlook.de. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

6. Children's Privacy

Both apps are professional engineering reference tools intended for drilling fluid engineers and related industry professionals. They are not directed at children, and in any event, we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (or under 16/18 depending on jurisdiction), in either app.

7. International Data Transfers

Not applicable to either app — no data is transmitted anywhere. All processing happens locally on your own device.

8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Neither app uses cookies, web beacons, mobile advertising IDs, or any similar tracking technology.

9. Third-Party Services

Neither app integrates any third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud services. If you download either app through an app store (Apple App Store, Google Play, or another distributor), that store's own privacy policy governs the purchase/download transaction itself — this Privacy Policy covers only the apps' own behavior after installation.

10. Data Retention

Local preferences and saved calculation history (Section 2) remain on your device until you clear them or uninstall the app, in both apps. We hold no copy of this data and cannot retrieve, back up, or delete it on your behalf, since it never reaches us.

11. Security

Because no calculation data leaves your device in either app, the primary risk surface is your own device's security, which remains your responsibility (e.g., device passcode, OS updates). We do not operate any server or database that could be breached in connection with either app.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect a new feature or a change in law. The "Last updated" date above will be revised accordingly. Material changes will be reflected in the version of this document distributed with a new app update.
