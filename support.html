Frequently asked

Does the app work offline?
Yes. Mud Engineer Pro works fully offline — no signal needed on the rig, and no data leaves your phone.

Can I switch between US and Metric units?
Yes. You can switch between US oilfield and Metric units with one tap.

Is there a subscription?
No. Mud Engineer Pro is a one-time purchase — no subscription and no ads.
